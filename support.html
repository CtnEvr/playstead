About PlayStead

PlayStead is a collection of 12 offline games for iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account or internet connection to play.

Common Questions

Does PlayStead require an internet connection?
No. PlayStead is designed to work offline.

Does PlayStead contain ads?
No. PlayStead does not contain advertising.

Does PlayStead require a subscription?
No. PlayStead does not use subscriptions.

Can I play on both iPhone and iPad?
Yes. PlayStead is designed for both iPhone and iPad.

Does PlayStead require an account?
No. No account or sign-in is required.
