Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

PlayStead is designed to work offline and with privacy in mind. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

Data Collection

PlayStead does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not use:

  • Advertising
  • Third-party analytics
  • Tracking technologies
  • User accounts
  • Location services
  • Contact information
  • Social media integrations

Local Game Data

Game progress, high scores, settings, and other gameplay-related information may be stored locally on your device so that PlayStead can preserve your progress and preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to PlayStead or to third parties.

Internet Access

PlayStead does not require an internet connection for gameplay. The app is designed as a self-contained offline game collection.

Third-Party Services

PlayStead does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, account, or social media services.

Some game content, such as dictionary data and licensed sound effects, may originate from third-party sources. These resources are bundled with the app and do not collect or transmit personal data.

Children's Privacy

PlayStead does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The app does not require users to provide personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PlayStead's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
